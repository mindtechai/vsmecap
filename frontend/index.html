VSMECAP — Enterprise-grade sustainability data infrastructure for SMEs and their value chains

Collect, structure, validate and securely exchange sustainability information using the VSME framework. Based on EFRAG VSME Dec 2024, EU 2025/1710, EU 2026/470. Reports structured for review, not audited, not certified by EFRAG.

Basic B1-B11 Partly automated and optional Comprehensive C1-C9 Guidance only. Upload utility bills provided by the user — processed locally in browser, only hash/metadata stored. No commercial supplier verification implied. UK: DESNZ, PPN 006, MPAN/MPRN. EU: DEFRA/IEA.

Basic module (B1-B11)

B1 Basis • B2 Practices • B3 Energy and GHG Scope 1-2 (drafts from bills) • B4 Pollution • B5 Biodiversity • B6 Water • B7 Resource/waste • B8 Workforce general • B9 Health & safety • B10 Remuneration • B11 Corruption

Comprehensive module (C1-C9, optional)

C1 Strategy • C2 Practices • C3 GHG targets • C4 Climate risks • C5 Workforce • C6 Human rights policies • C7 Severe incidents • C8 Sector revenues • C9 Gender diversity — Optional when customer, lender or investor asks for more than Basic.

How it works

1. Ingest — Upload bills, meter identifiers extracted. 2. Integrity — Source files listed alongside every figure. 3. Output — JSON, CSV, PDF. XBRL in development. Free tier ≤1000 employees, commercial >1000 + PPN 006.
